# Lio-Jotstar OTT Platform Analysis

## Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Codebasics Resume Project Challenge**.

The objective was to analyze the performance of **LioCinema** and **Jotstar**, two fictional OTT platforms preparing for a strategic merger. The analysis focuses on subscriber behavior, content consumption patterns, inactivity trends, and subscription upgrade/downgrade behavior to support data-driven decision-making.

## Business Problem

Lio, a leading telecommunications provider in India, is planning a merger with Jotstar, a popular streaming platform. Before the merger, management wanted to understand how both platforms performed during January–November 2024.

The goal was to identify opportunities to improve customer retention, content strategy, subscriber growth, and overall platform performance after the merger.

## Key Insights

* Identified subscriber segments with the highest engagement.
* Analyzed factors contributing to user inactivity.
* Evaluated upgrade and downgrade behavior across plans.
* Compared content performance between both platforms.
* Identified opportunities for improving customer retention and engagement.

## Business Recommendations

* Improve retention strategies for inactive subscribers.
* Expand high-performing content categories.
* Optimize subscription plans to reduce downgrades.
* Personalize recommendations based on user behavior.
* Focus marketing efforts on high-growth customer segments.
